Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

mmnt has no accounts and never asks for your name or email. Everything you write stays on your device and is never sent to a server in any form. The only things the app sends out are anonymous usage statistics and error reports, both of which you can turn off in Settings, and a request to check for new versions.

1. Your writing and settings

Your entries, writing history and statistics, and app settings are stored in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device. Finished entries are permanently deleted from your device after the retention period you set (30 days by default), except for entries you mark to keep. Uninstalling the app removes this data as well.

2. Anonymous usage statistics

To understand which features are used and how often, mmnt sends anonymous usage statistics. This is on by default and stops immediately when you turn off "Send anonymous usage stats" in Settings. Once off, no data is generated or queued.

This is everything that is sent:

Install IDA random value created on first launch. It cannot identify you or your device, and a reinstall produces a new one.
App version, platformFor example 0.1.0, macos
OS major versionFor example 26. The exact version is not sent.
Usage eventsThe kind of action taken, such as opening the app, starting or finishing an entry, or changing a setting.
Bucketed valuesEntry length and writing time are sent only as ranges such as "50–149 words" or "3–5 minutes", never as exact numbers.

The text of your entries or any part of it, exact character and word counts, your name, email, device name, locale, and location are never sent. Statistics are stored with PostHog on servers in the EU. No per-person profiles are created, and IP addresses are configured to be discarded on receipt.

3. Processing by a third party and international transfer

Storage and analysis of the statistics and error reports above are handled by PostHog, and the data is kept on servers outside Korea. Turning off "Send anonymous usage stats" in Settings stops this transfer as well.

RecipientPostHog, Inc. (privacy policy)

When and howSent periodically over the internet while the app is in use

RetentionOne year from collection. No per-person profiles are created.

4. Error reports

If an unhandled error occurs inside the app, the error type, its message, and a stack trace showing where in the code it happened are sent through the same channel as the statistics above. Home folder paths that may appear in the message are masked before sending, and your writing is never included. Turning off "Send anonymous usage stats" also turns off error reports.

5. Update checks

On launch, and when you check manually in Settings, the app downloads a small version file hosted on GitHub. The app adds no information to this request, but as with any internet connection, GitHub may log connection details such as your IP address. The GitHub Privacy Statement applies to that.

6. This website

This site is served by GitHub Pages and uses no analytics tools or cookies. The version shown on the download button is filled in by reading the same version file on GitHub, so the same GitHub connection logging may apply.

7. What is not collected

  • Accounts, names, email addresses, phone numbers
  • The text of your entries or any raw values derived from it
  • Device name, exact OS version, language and region settings
  • Location, contacts, or data from other apps

8. Retention and deletion

Because the anonymous statistics contain nothing that links them to you, it is not possible to single out and delete one person's data. If you no longer want to send them, turn the setting off. Uninstalling the app discards the install ID, so nothing afterwards can be connected to it.

9. Terms of use

mmnt comes with no warranty of any kind. By design, entries are deleted once their retention period passes, so please mark anything you need to keep or copy it elsewhere. The developer accepts no liability for damages arising from use of the app, including loss of writing due to software errors or device problems.

10. Privacy officer

Questions about personal data, requests to access, correct or delete it, and complaints are handled directly by the developer of mmnt. Email mmnt.writing@gmail.com and you will get a prompt reply. General questions that are fine to discuss in public can also go to GitHub Issues.

11. Where to seek help

If you need to report or discuss a privacy concern, you can contact the authorities below. If you live outside Korea, you may also contact the data protection authority in your country.

  • Personal Information Infringement Report Center (KISA): 118 within Korea, privacy.kisa.or.kr
  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee: +82-1833-6972, kopico.go.kr

12. Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the effective date on this page is updated. Changes that add to what is collected are also noted in the app's update notes. For questions, open an issue on GitHub Issues or email mmnt.writing@gmail.com.
